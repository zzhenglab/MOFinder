## Process-detail-enriched control dataset

As an auxiliary control alongside the primary eight-variable dataset, we prepared a process-enriched representation adding vessel type, vessel capacity (mL), and agitation. Cleaning preserves all 15,340 positive and 15,063 inferred negative records. The same normalization and category mapping are applied to both classes, with original descriptions retained for audit.

Vessel names were consolidated across spelling, punctuation, and material variants. Unspecified types and classes with fewer than 10 positive records were assigned to Not reported; this category therefore includes rare known vessels. Capacities were accepted only from interpretable vessel sizes, without substituting solution volumes or inferring missing measurements. Nine agitation categories distinguish stirring, sonication, and shaking or mixing methods. Labels contain two to five words; specific methods and timing remain in the detailed audit. Targeted source-PDF checks addressed ambiguous descriptions; the source audit records the evidence and remaining uncertainty. Unresolved agitation was assigned to Not reported without assuming static conditions. Stirred before main synthesis denotes preparatory mixing before the main heating or aging step; later conditions remain unspecified. Stirring reported does not establish stirring throughout the reaction.

Among positive records, PTFE-lined autoclaves account for 5,734 (37.4%), vials for 2,584 (16.8%), and vessel Not reported for 2,487 (16.2%; Figure Sxx). Numeric capacities are available for 7,271 records (47.4%), with a median of 23 mL and an interquartile range of 16–25 mL; 7,768 capacities are Not reported and 301 are Ambiguous (Figure Sxx+1). The No stirring and Stirred before static synthesis categories account for 6,522 (42.5%) and 1,759 (11.5%) positive records, respectively; agitation is Not reported for 3,886 (25.3%; Figure Sxx+2).

The figures summarize positive records and unique DOIs; categorical DOI counts allow each paper to contribute to multiple categories, while capacity summaries use one median per DOI. The enriched training and holdout files preserve the original 23,528 and 2,595 examples, respectively, including row order and labels, changing only the three additional inputs and the system-prompt input list. Process annotations in inferred negatives may derive from successful parent protocols and are not independently observed failed-trial measurements. Washing and activation remain outside this crystallization-outcome control. These distributions describe feature availability and do not establish improved predictive performance.
